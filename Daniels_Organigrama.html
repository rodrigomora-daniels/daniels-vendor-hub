How to read this:
• Levels go top to bottom: Board → CEO and CMO → direct reports → teams. Solid lines are direct reporting lines; dashed purple lines are indirect lines (Rodrigo for the areas that report to Ted).
• Dashed blue box = co-leaders who share one team. That team reports to both, depending on shift.
• Floors (labels on the left) put the same level on the same row: area managers and buyers, then store managers and supervisors, then associates and staff. Boxes with a + have a team: click to zoom in one level; use Zoom out or press Esc to go back. When zoomed in, scroll up to see the reporting line above.
• Purple note = second reporting line (Karen for Angel). Gray italic note = indirect, often temporary, line (Rey for Ana and Letty). A + in a corner means that box has a team inside.
